## Placement — read this before moving a camp

Regenerate with `Custom/Documentation/scripts/gen_hyjal_camps.py` (edit its
`CAMPS` table); do not hand-edit `10_faction_bases.sql`.

| Camp | Centre | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Jaina's Encampment | 4443.50, −2077.47, **1206.25** | verified in-game |
| Thrall's Vanguard | — | **pending a verified coordinate** |

Rules, all learned from the first pass floating:

1. **A centre is only valid if `.gps` reports `GroundZ == FloorZ`.** Where they
   differ there is a VMAP object above the terrain, and anything placed there
   either stands on the object or clips inside it. The scouted Horde area
   (5660.58, −2980.15) reads GroundZ **1546.50** / FloorZ **1560.70** — a 14-yard
   gap — so neither height is safe to build on.
2. **Take the reading standing on the ground, not flying.** A flying player's z
   is metres above `FloorZ` and produces a camp in mid-air.
3. **Keep rings tight** (13 yd service / 16 yd guards). The Grove of Aessina
   shelf is flat to within ~0.6 yd out to 13 yd but climbs to 1210.8 by 20 yd
   westward — the original 18 yd ring ran half the town onto that slope.
4. **Never anchor on arbitrary creatures.** Hyjal Bear Cubs sit *in* Climbing
   Trees, so their z is tree height. Service NPCs are always ground-placed and
   are the right reference: around the Alliance centre they read 1206.05–1206.67.

Until the Horde coordinate lands, `10_faction_bases.sql`, `11_graveyards.sql`
and `30_entry_points.sql` all deliberately omit Thrall's Vanguard rather than
ship a camp, a graveyard (a forced teleport) or a teleporter entry that drops
players through the floor. Horde players use the neutral Hyjal graveyards.
